Where your answers point

SASE architectures differ on two things. First, where enforcement runs: in a vendor's central cloud, or locally across your users, sites, and workloads. Second, who operates it: the vendor, or you. Together they set how much sovereignty you keep, meaning control over where your traffic, logs, and keys are handled.
