How to read this

No architecture type is the right answer on its own, and most environments end up as a blend. Look at the circles across the rows you checked: a type with mostly full circles there is worth a closer look, and a type with empty circles on requirements you care about is worth questioning hard. Give extra weight to sovereignty, performance, and resilience, since those are the hardest to change later.
